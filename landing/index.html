What you get back

Four endpoints. No dashboard to log into, no CSV to parse, no scraping to maintain.

Viability score

A 0–100 opportunity score per SKU, with a term-by-term explanation of how it was reached — not a black box.

Landed cost

China unit cost as a range, plus freight and duty to a US door, so margin is computed against what you actually pay.

Risk flags

Compliance, IP, oversized, battery, thin margin. The reasons a shipment gets held or a listing gets pulled.

Provenance on every field

Each datapoint carries a source URL, an observation timestamp and a confidence score. If we cannot source it, we say so instead of guessing.
